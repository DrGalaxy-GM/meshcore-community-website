What Is This Device I Found?

MeshCore repeaters are small radio devices that help relay text messages between people using the network. They may be mounted on rooftops, poles, hillsides, or other elevated locations to improve their range.

What Does MeshCore Accomplish?

MeshCore enables people to exchange short text messages over radio, without depending on cellular towers or internet service. Battery- and solar-powered equipment can keep parts of the network operating during power outages.

📱

Off-Grid Messaging

Exchange text messages between compatible devices, even without a phone signal or Wi-Fi connection.

🔁

Extended Coverage

Repeaters forward messages across the network, potentially reaching people beyond the range of a single radio.

🤝

Community Resilience

Independently operated radio devices can provide another communication option during emergencies.

Coverage depends on the available repeaters, terrain, radio conditions, and network configuration. MeshCore is not a replacement for 911 or official emergency services.
