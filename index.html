Found a Small Box With an Antenna?

It may be part of MeshCore — a community-supported radio network that helps people stay connected even when cell service and internet connections are unavailable.

What Is This Device? How Does MeshCore Help?

What Is This Device I Found?

MeshCore repeaters are small radio devices that help relay text messages between people using the network. They may be mounted on rooftops, poles, hillsides, or other elevated locations to improve their range.

☀️

Solar-Powered Repeaters

Some repeaters use small solar panels and rechargeable batteries so they can operate independently of the electrical grid.

📡

Weatherproof Repeaters

These may look like small sealed boxes with an antenna attached. Their purpose is to extend radio coverage.

🏔️

Remote Repeaters

Some are placed in elevated or remote locations where radio signals can reach more of the surrounding community.


Not a surveillance camera. A standard, purpose-built MeshCore repeater relays digital radio messages. It does not need a camera, microphone, or license-plate reader to perform its function.

Please leave identified communications equipment undisturbed. Disconnecting a repeater can interrupt service for other network users.

What Does MeshCore Accomplish?

MeshCore enables people to exchange short text messages over radio, without depending on cellular towers or internet service. Battery- and solar-powered equipment can keep parts of the network operating during power outages.

📱

Off-Grid Messaging

Exchange text messages between compatible devices, even without a phone signal or Wi-Fi connection.

🔁

Extended Coverage

Repeaters forward messages across the network, potentially reaching people beyond the range of a single radio.

🤝

Community Resilience

Independently operated radio devices can provide another communication option during emergencies.

Coverage depends on the available repeaters, terrain, radio conditions, and network configuration. MeshCore is not a replacement for 911 or official emergency services.
