Found a Small Box With an Antenna?

You may have discovered a MeshCore repeater — a small radio device that helps people stay connected when conventional communications are unavailable.

What Is a MeshCore Repeater?

A MeshCore repeater receives and retransmits short digital radio messages between compatible devices. Repeaters extend the reach of a community-supported network that operates without requiring cell towers or internet service.

Repeaters come in different shapes and sizes. Some have solar panels, some are housed in weatherproof enclosures, and others are compact devices with antennas.

📡 Community Communications — Not a Surveillance Camera

A standard purpose-built MeshCore repeater does not need a camera, microphone, or license-plate reader to relay text messages.

Please leave identified communications equipment undisturbed. Disconnecting or removing a repeater can interrupt communications for other people using the network.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a camera or license-plate reader?

No. A standard MeshCore repeater relays digital radio messages. It does not photograph people or read license plates.

Does it record conversations?

No. A standard purpose-built MeshCore repeater does not record spoken conversations. It processes digital radio messages, and message handling depends on the device and its configuration.

Does it need the internet or cell towers?

No. MeshCore radio communications can operate independently of cellular and internet infrastructure.

Can it operate during a power outage?

Yes. Repeaters with functioning battery or solar power can continue operating without grid electricity.

Is the device monitored?

Maybe. Some operators remotely check their repeaters' status and network activity. This does not necessarily mean the device has GPS tracking or is continuously monitored.

Is it an official public safety system?

No. MeshCore is a community radio technology, not an official emergency services system. It can provide an additional communications option, but it is not a replacement for 911.

Can I use MeshCore myself?

Yes. With compatible equipment and access to a participating network, you can exchange text messages with other users.

Why are repeaters installed in elevated or remote locations?

Higher locations often provide better radio coverage by reducing obstructions. This helps messages travel farther and reach more participants.

Help Keep Your Community Connected

MeshCore networks depend on participating people and functioning repeaters. Leaving equipment undisturbed helps preserve communications for others.
